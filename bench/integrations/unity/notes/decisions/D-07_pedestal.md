# D-07: display pedestal

Date: 2026-10-02. Status: current. Supersedes D-03.

The pedestal is Unity's built-in cylinder with a diameter of 0.60 m and a height of 0.90 m. Its base stands on the floor (y = 0), centred on the origin. Material name `PedestalMatte` on the Standard shader, albedo RGB 0.20, 0.45, 0.70, smoothness 0.2. The object is named `Pedestal`.
