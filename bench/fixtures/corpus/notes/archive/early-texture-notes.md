# Early texture format notes

Three formats were tried in the first weeks: raw RGBA, a palette format and 8 bit grayscale.

The palette format looked fine on flat map areas and failed on gradients. Raw RGBA used four times the memory. Grayscale could not carry colour. No decision yet, tests continue next week.
