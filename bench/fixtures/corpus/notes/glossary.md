# Glossary

- Tile: a square image of one map region at one zoom level.
- Pinned set: tiles in the viewport that the cache never drops.
- Frame graph: the list of passes that make up one frame.
- Checkpoint: a saved position of the ingest worker.
- Dead letter queue: where records that fail validation wait for review.
