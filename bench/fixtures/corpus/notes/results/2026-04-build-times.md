# Build times with the remote cache, April 2026

Median CI run time over two weeks of merges:

- With the remote cache: 6.5 minutes.
- Without it: 14.2 minutes.

Cache hit rate over the same period was 81 percent. Most misses came from the bundle step after changes to the shared shader folder.
